### 第 1 章｜开场：这个国家几乎全是空的（*A Country That Looks Empty*）

**V2 词数** 97 ｜ **135 wpm** 43 s ｜ **140 wpm** 42 s ｜ **150 wpm** 39 s

**1.1**

> This is Canada. Ten million square kilometres of land, and from up here, almost none of it looks lived in.

*这就是加拿大。一千万平方公里的土地，而从高空看下去，几乎没有任何一处像是有人居住。*

**1.2**

> Now watch the six largest population centres in the country light up. These are the biggest built-up areas in Canada, as Statistics Canada counts them. Between any two of them, the median distance is about two thousand seven hundred kilometres.

*现在看加拿大最大的六个人口中心依次亮起。这是按加拿大统计局口径计算的全国最大的六片连片建成区。它们两两之间的距离，中位数约为 2,700 公里。*

**1.3**

> The closest of those six are still a hundred and fifty-seven kilometres apart. The farthest are more than three thousand kilometres apart.

*这六个人口中心里，最近的两个也相隔 157 公里；最远的两个相隔三千公里以上。*

**1.4**

> The six largest, and not one of them next to another. So where is everybody?

*最大的六个，没有一个挨着另一个。那么，人都到哪里去了？*

### 第 2 章｜反转：两个方块（*Two Squares*）

**V2 词数** 157 ｜ **135 wpm** 70 s ｜ **140 wpm** 67 s ｜ **150 wpm** 63 s

**2.1**

> Here is the answer, and it is stranger than the question. Lay two squares, two hundred kilometres on a side, over this country. One over Toronto. One over Montreal. Together they cover about eighty thousand square kilometres. Inside them live forty-three percent of everyone in a Canadian population centre.

*答案在这里，而且比问题更奇怪。把两个边长 200 公里的方块盖在这个国家上：一个盖在多伦多，一个盖在蒙特利尔。它们合起来约八万平方公里。而住在里面的，是加拿大全部人口中心人口的 43%。*

**2.2**

> And the two squares are not even copies of each other. The Toronto square contains eighty-nine population centres and eight and a half million people. The Montreal one contains ninety-six centres and four and a half million. More centres, half the people.

*而且这两个方块彼此并不相同。多伦多那个方块里有 89 个人口中心、850 万人；蒙特利尔那个方块里有 96 个人口中心、450 万人。中心更多，人口只有一半。*

**2.3**

> The provinces tell the same story. Ontario holds forty-one percent of the total, Quebec twenty-three, British Columbia fourteen. That is seventy-eight percent in three provinces. Of the thirteen provinces and territories, ten hold less than four percent each.

*各省的数据讲的是同一个故事。安大略占 41%，魁北克占 23%，不列颠哥伦比亚占 14%。三个省就占了 78%。在 13 个省和地区中，有 10 个各自占比不到 4%。*

**2.4**

> So the population is not spread across the country. It is stacked, very high, in a very small number of places. And those places are in the east.

*所以人口并不是铺开在这个国家上的，而是非常高地堆叠在极少数几个地方。而这些地方都在东部。*

### 第 3 章｜一条走廊，两种性质（*One Corridor, Two Different Things*）

**V2 词数** 205 ｜ **135 wpm** 91 s ｜ **140 wpm** 88 s ｜ **150 wpm** 82 s

**3.1**

> And the east means one corridor: southern Ontario down to Quebec City, following the St. Lawrence. Inside the map we drew for it, four hundred and fourteen population centres hold fifty-nine percent of the total.

*而所谓东部，指的是同一条走廊：从安大略南部沿圣劳伦斯河，一直到魁北克城。在我们为这条走廊划定的地图范围内，414 个人口中心占了总数的 59%。*

**3.2**

> But it is not one uniform band. It has three peaks: Toronto, Ottawa-Gatineau, and Montreal. Between Toronto and Ottawa, the band thins out, and that stretch is the weakest point anywhere along it.

*但它并不是一条均匀的带。它有三个尖峰：多伦多、渥太华-加蒂诺、蒙特利尔。在多伦多与渥太华之间，这条带明显变薄，而这一段是整条走廊最弱的地方。*

**3.3**

> Slice the corridor into bands half a degree of longitude wide, and the pattern reverses. West of Toronto, four of those bands together hold a hundred and nine population centres. Toronto's own band holds thirty-one. Yet Toronto's band has more than twice the people.

*把这条走廊按经度切成半度宽的带，格局会反转。在多伦多以西，四个这样的带合起来有 109 个人口中心；而多伦多自己那一个带有 31 个。但多伦多那一个带的人口，是那四个带合计的两倍以上。*

**3.4**

> Now measure it properly. Sample points along the line from Toronto to Ottawa, every five kilometres. Just over twenty-one percent of those points have no population centre within twenty-five kilometres. The largest single gap is fifty-two kilometres wide.

*现在来精确测量。沿着多伦多到渥太华的连线，每隔 5 公里取一个采样点。其中略超过 21% 的采样点，周围 25 公里内没有任何人口中心。最大的单处空缺宽达 52 公里。*

**3.5**

> Then the line turns to Montreal, and the gap disappears. Across a hundred and fifty-seven kilometres, not one sample point is beyond the limit. And Montreal to Quebec City: nothing. Zero percent, across two hundred and thirty-two kilometres.

*然后这条线转向蒙特利尔——空缺消失了。在一百五十七公里里，没有一个采样点越限。蒙特利尔到魁北克城同样：在二百三十二公里里，也是零。*

**3.6**

> Two neighbouring segments of the same corridor. Twenty-one percent, and zero percent. Nobody has yet explained why.

*同一条走廊上相邻的两段：21%，和 0%。目前还没有人解释这是为什么。*

### 第 4 章｜另一半：北部（*The Other Half*）

**V2 词数** 152 ｜ **135 wpm** 68 s ｜ **140 wpm** 65 s ｜ **150 wpm** 61 s

**4.1**

> Now fly north of the sixtieth parallel. The three northern territories hold twelve population centres and sixty-nine thousand people. North of the line, there are fourteen, with seventy-one thousand five hundred and ninety-two people. Two-tenths of one percent of everyone living in a population centre in Canada.

*现在飞到北纬 60 度以北。三个北方地区加起来有 12 个人口中心、六万九千人；而这条纬度线以北是 14 个中心、71,592 人——相当于加拿大全部人口中心人口的千分之二。*

**4.2**

> These are real places. Whitehorse, Yellowknife, Iqaluit. Fourteen of them, with stores and schools and airports, spread across the northern third of the country. The nearest neighbour of Kuujjuaq is five hundred and fifty kilometres away. Inuvik's is five hundred and forty. Whitehorse's is four hundred and thirty. Up here, the nearest town is often in another territory, or another province.

*这些是真实存在的地方：怀特霍斯、耶洛奈夫、伊卡卢伊特。共 14 处，有商店、有学校、有机场，散布在这个国家北部的三分之一里。库朱阿克最近的邻居在 550 公里外，伊努维克的是 540 公里，怀特霍斯的是 430 公里。在这里，最近的城镇往往在另一个地区，甚至另一个省。*

**4.3**

> What we can say is where these places are, and how many people live in them. What we cannot say is why the pattern looks this way. Answering that would take data this film does not have, and so it stays an open question.

*我们能说的是：这些地方在哪里、住着多少人。我们不能说的是：为什么格局会是这样。要回答那个问题，需要本片没有的数据，所以它仍然是一个开放问题。*

### 第 5 章｜我们知道什么，不知道什么（*What This Map Does and Doesn't Say*）

**V2 词数** 149 ｜ **135 wpm** 66 s ｜ **140 wpm** 64 s ｜ **150 wpm** 60 s

**5.1**

> So here is everything we measured. Two squares: forty-three percent. Ontario and Quebec: sixty-three percent. One corridor in the east: fifty-nine percent. Three different measurements, three different scales, all pointing the same way.

*那么，这就是我们测到的全部。两个方块：43%。安大略与魁北克：63%。东部的一条走廊：59%。三个不同的测量、三个不同的尺度，指向同一个方向。*

**5.2**

> And the opposite is also true. Take the thirty-four largest population centres, spread right across the country, and the distance between any two of them is still about one thousand four hundred kilometres.

*反过来也成立。取 34 个最大的人口中心，它们散布在全国各地，而任意两个之间的距离中位数仍有约 1,400 公里。*

**5.3**

> One last thing, and it matters. Thirty million, three hundred and eighty-nine thousand, nine hundred and ninety-nine. That is the combined population of one thousand and twenty-six population centres. It is not the population of Canada. People live outside these boundaries too, and this map does not include them.

*最后一点，这一点很重要。30,389,999。这是 1,026 个人口中心的人口总和。它不是加拿大的人口。也有人住这些边界之外，而这张地图没有把他们算进来。*

**5.4**

> So this map tells us where people are. It does not tell us why they are there. Measuring the pattern is the easy part. Explaining it is the problem we are still stuck on.

*所以这张地图告诉我们人在哪里，却没有告诉我们他们为什么在那里。把这个格局测量出来是容易的部分；解释它，才是我们至今卡住的问题。*
