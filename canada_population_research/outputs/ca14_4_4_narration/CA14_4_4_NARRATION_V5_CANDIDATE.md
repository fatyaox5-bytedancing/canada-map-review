## 二、旁白正文（英文 + 中文对照）

### 第 1 章｜开场：这个国家几乎全是空的（*A Country That Looks Empty*）

**V5 词数** 115 ｜ **135 wpm** 51 s ｜ **140 wpm** 49 s ｜ **150 wpm** 46 s

**1.1**

> This is Canada. Ten million square kilometres of land, and from up here, almost none of it looks lived in.

*这就是加拿大。一千万平方公里的土地，而从高空看下去，几乎没有任何一处像是有人居住。*

**1.2**

> Now watch the six largest population centres in the country light up. A population centre is where people are concentrated — as Statistics Canada counts them. Between any two of these six there are fifteen distances, and the median of those fifteen is about two thousand seven hundred kilometres. Not the closest pair. The middle one.

*现在看加拿大最大的六个人口中心依次亮起。人口中心就是人口聚集的地方——按加拿大统计局的界定。这六个中心两两之间一共有 15 组距离，这个中位数约为 2,700 公里。它不是最近的那一对，而是正中间的那一对。*

**1.3**

> The closest of those six are still a hundred and fifty-seven kilometres apart. The farthest are more than three thousand kilometres apart.

*这六个人口中心里，最近的两个也相隔 157 公里；最远的两个相隔三千公里以上。*

**1.4**

> The six largest are spread far apart, from the Pacific coast to the Atlantic. So where is everybody?

*最大的六个彼此相距遥远，从太平洋沿岸一直到大西洋沿岸。那么，人都到哪里去了？*

### 第 2 章｜反转：两个方块（*Two Squares*）

**V5 词数** 152 ｜ **135 wpm** 68 s ｜ **140 wpm** 65 s ｜ **150 wpm** 61 s

**2.1**

> Here is the answer, and it is stranger than the question. Lay two squares, two hundred kilometres on a side, over this country. One over Toronto. One over Montreal. Together they cover about eighty thousand square kilometres. Inside them live forty-three percent of everyone in a Canadian population centre.

*答案在这里，而且比问题更奇怪。把两个边长 200 公里的方块盖在这个国家上：一个盖在多伦多，一个盖在蒙特利尔。它们合起来约八万平方公里。而住在里面的，是加拿大全部人口中心人口的 43%。*

**2.2**

> And the two squares are not even copies of each other. The Toronto square contains eighty-nine population centres and eight and a half million people. The Montreal one contains ninety-six centres and four and a half million. More centres, half the people.

*而且这两个方块彼此并不相同。多伦多那个方块里有 89 个人口中心、850 万人；蒙特利尔那个方块里有 96 个人口中心、450 万人。中心更多，人口只有一半。*

**2.3**

> The provinces agree. Ontario holds forty-one percent of that total, Quebec twenty-three, British Columbia fourteen. Those shares are of the combined population of the one thousand and twenty-six population centres, not of Canada. Together, the three provinces hold seventy-eight percent.

*各省的数据一致。安大略占 41%、魁北克占 23%、不列颠哥伦比亚占 14%——这些比例的分母是 1,026 个人口中心的合计人口，**不是加拿大全国人口**。三个省合起来占 78%。*

**2.4**

> So the population is not spread across the country. It is stacked, very high, in a very small number of places.

*所以人口并不是铺开在这个国家上的，而是非常高地堆叠在极少数几个地方。*

### 第 3 章｜一条走廊，两种性质（*One Corridor, Two Different Things*）

**V5 词数** 354 ｜ **135 wpm** 157 s ｜ **140 wpm** 152 s ｜ **150 wpm** 142 s

**3.1**

> Those places run in a corridor: population centres strung across southern Ontario and Quebec, following the St. Lawrence. Inside the map we drew for that corridor, four hundred and fourteen population centres hold about fifty-nine percent. That is a share of the people in Canada's population centres — not of the country's population, and not of the number of centres. But three of the six largest are far to the west.

*这些地方连成一条走廊：人口中心沿圣劳伦斯河，散布在安大略南部与魁北克。在我们为这条走廊划定的地图范围内，414 个人口中心占了约 59%——这个比例的分母是加拿大人口中心的合计人口，既不是加拿大全国人口，也不是人口中心的个数。但六个最大中心里有三个远在西部。*

**3.2**

> The corridor is not one uniform band. It has three peaks: Toronto, Ottawa-Gatineau, and Montreal. Between Toronto and Ottawa, the band thins out, and that stretch is the weakest point anywhere along it.

*这条走廊并不是一条均匀的带。它有三个尖峰：多伦多、渥太华-加蒂诺、蒙特利尔。在多伦多与渥太华之间，这条带明显变薄，而这一段是整条走廊最弱的地方。*

**3.3**

> Slice the corridor into bands half a degree of longitude wide, and the sizes change sharply. West of Toronto, four of those bands together hold a hundred and nine population centres; Toronto's own band holds thirty-one. Yet Toronto's band has more than twice the people. Its centres average about two hundred thousand people each; in the four bands to the west, about twenty-seven thousand. That average is pulled up by a few very large centres — Toronto itself accounts for five and a half million of the six and a quarter million. The median population centre in that band is not much bigger than the median one to the west.

*把这条走廊按经度切成半度宽的带，规模差异立刻显现。在多伦多以西，四个这样的带合起来有 109 个人口中心；而多伦多自己那一个带有 31 个。但多伦多那一个带的人口是那四个带合计的两倍以上——它每个中心的平均规模约 20 万人，而那四个带只有约 2.7 万人。这个平均值是被少数几个极大的中心拉高的：仅多伦多一处，就占了那 625 万人里的 550 万。那个带里的中位人口中心，比西侧的中位中心大不了多少。*

**3.4**

> Now measure it properly. Sample points along the line from Toronto to Ottawa, every five kilometres. Just over twenty-one percent of those points have no population centre within twenty-five kilometres. The largest single gap is fifty-two kilometres wide.

*现在来精确测量。沿着多伦多到渥太华的连线，每隔 5 公里取一个采样点。其中略超过 21% 的采样点，周围 25 公里内没有任何人口中心。最大的单处空缺宽达 52 公里。*

**3.5**

> Then the line turns to Montreal, and the picture changes. Sampling the same way from Ottawa to Montreal, across a hundred and fifty-seven kilometres, none of the thirty-two sample points has its nearest population centre beyond twenty-five kilometres. And from Montreal to Quebec City, over two hundred and thirty-two kilometres, again none of the forty-seven. Along those two stretches, every sample point stays within twenty-five kilometres of some population centre. That measures the spacing of these centres. It does not mean the land between them is empty.

*然后这条线转向蒙特利尔，情况就不同了。从渥太华到蒙特利尔，以同样方式在一百五十七公里上采样，32 个采样点中没有一个的最近人口中心超过 25 公里。再从蒙特利尔到魁北克城，在二百三十二公里上同样没有：47 个采样点中一个也没有。在这两段上，每一个采样点与某个人口中心的距离都在 25 公里以内。这测的是这些中心之间的间距，**并不意味着它们之间的土地是空的**。*

**3.6**

> Two neighbouring segments of the same corridor. Twenty-one percent, and zero percent. Nobody has yet explained why.

*同一条走廊上相邻的两段：21%，和 0%。目前还没有人解释这是为什么。*

### 第 4 章｜另一半：北部（*The Other Half*）

**V5 词数** 162 ｜ **135 wpm** 72 s ｜ **140 wpm** 69 s ｜ **150 wpm** 65 s

**4.1**

> Now fly north of the sixtieth parallel. Two different ways of defining the north give two different counts. The three northern territories hold twelve population centres and sixty-nine thousand people. North of the line, there are fourteen, with seventy-one thousand five hundred and ninety-two people — about zero point two four percent of everyone living in a population centre in Canada.

*现在飞到北纬 60 度以北。对「北方」的两种不同界定，会给出两套不同的数字。三个北方地区加起来有 12 个人口中心、六万九千人；而这条纬度线以北是 14 个中心、71,592 人——相当于加拿大全部人口中心人口的约 0.24%。*

**4.2**

> These are real places. Whitehorse, Yellowknife, Iqaluit. Fourteen of them, with stores and schools and airports, spread across Canada's far north. The nearest neighbour of Kuujjuaq is five hundred and fifty kilometres away. Inuvik's is five hundred and forty. Whitehorse's is four hundred and thirty. Up here, the nearest town is often in another territory, or another province.

*这些是真实存在的地方：怀特霍斯、耶洛奈夫、伊卡卢伊特。共 14 处，有商店、有学校、有机场，散布在加拿大的极北地区。库朱阿克最近的邻居在 550 公里外，伊努维克的是 540 公里，怀特霍斯的是 430 公里。在这里，最近的城镇往往在另一个地区，甚至另一个省。*

**4.3**

> What we can say is where these places are, and how many people live in them. What we cannot say is why the pattern looks this way. Answering that would take data this film does not have, and so it stays an open question.

*我们能说的是：这些地方在哪里、住着多少人。我们不能说的是：为什么格局会是这样。要回答那个问题，需要本片没有的数据，所以它仍然是一个开放问题。*

### 第 5 章｜我们知道什么，不知道什么（*What This Map Does and Doesn't Say*）

**V5 词数** 175 ｜ **135 wpm** 78 s ｜ **140 wpm** 75 s ｜ **150 wpm** 70 s

**5.1**

> So here is everything we measured. Two squares: forty-three percent. Ontario and Quebec: sixty-three percent. One corridor in the east: about fifty-nine percent of the people in Canada's population centres. Three different measurements, three different scales, all pointing the same way.

*那么，这就是我们测到的全部。两个方块：43%。安大略与魁北克：63%。东部的一条走廊：占加拿大人口中心人口的约 59%。三个不同的测量、三个不同的尺度，指向同一个方向。*

**5.2**

> And the opposite is also true. Take the thirty-four largest population centres, spread right across the country. Pair them up every possible way — five hundred and sixty-one pairs — and the median distance across all of them is still about one thousand four hundred kilometres. Not the nearest pairs. The middle one.

*反过来也成立。取 34 个最大的人口中心，它们散布在全国各地。把它们按所有可能的方式两两配对——一共 561 对——这 561 组距离的中位数仍有约 1,400 公里。不是最近的那些配对，而是正中间的那一对。*

**5.3**

> One last thing, and it matters. Thirty million, three hundred and eighty-nine thousand, nine hundred and ninety-nine. That is the combined population of one thousand and twenty-six population centres. It is not the population of Canada. People live outside these boundaries too, and this map does not include them.

*最后一点，这一点很重要。30,389,999。这是 1,026 个人口中心的人口总和。它不是加拿大的人口。也有人住这些边界之外，而这张地图没有把他们算进来。*

**5.4**

> So this map tells us where people are. It does not tell us why they are there. Measuring the pattern is the easy part. Explaining it is the problem we are still stuck on.

*所以这张地图告诉我们人在哪里，却没有告诉我们他们为什么在那里。把这个格局测量出来是容易的部分；解释它，才是我们至今卡住的问题。*
